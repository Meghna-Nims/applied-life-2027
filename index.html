Explore Beyond Classrooms Through
Industrial Visits, Science Events
& Educational Tours

Gain practical exposure through industrial visits, National Science Day celebrations, educational tours and interactive learning experiences that connect classroom knowledge with real-world applications.

Industrial Visit to Saras Dairy, Jaipur

On 19th February 2025, students visited Saras Dairy, Jaipur, a unit of Rajasthan Cooperative Dairy Federation (RCDF). The visit provided insights into dairy production processes, quality control, plant operations, and supply chain management.

Educational Visit to CSWRI, Avikanagar

On 31st January 2025, students visited the Central Sheep and Wool Research Institute (CSWRI), Avikanagar, Rajasthan. The visit offered insights into research in sheep farming and wool production, including advanced techniques in breed improvement, wool processing, and sheep health management.

National Workshop on Advanced Molecular Techniques

The Institute organised a National Workshop in collaboration with ECH Incubation Center, University of Rajasthan, Jaipur. The workshop provided hands-on training and conceptual insights into advanced molecular techniques for 50 M.Sc. Biotechnology students, covering applications in biotechnology, medical diagnostics, and genetic research.

Industrial Visit to a Food Processing Facility

Students visited a food industry facility at Udaipuriya, Rajasthan, to gain practical exposure to industrial operations. The visit provided an opportunity to interact with industry professionals and understand workplace practices, production operations, and quality management in the food industry.
